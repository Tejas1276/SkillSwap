1. TULIKA — Opening, College Problem & Barter Vision 0:00 – 2:30 (2.5 Mins)
SCREEN CUE: Show Screen 1 (Landing Page)
"Good morning respected teachers and fellow students. My name is Tulika, and along with my teammates Varun, Tejas, and Ujjwal, we are proud to present our project: Skill Swap — a peer-to-peer collaborative learning platform built for college students.

In college, whenever we want to pick up a new technical or creative skill — like React development, Figma UI/UX, or public speaking — we face two big problems:
1. Expensive recorded video courses that cost thousands of rupees, where no instructor answers our personal doubts.
2. Watching YouTube tutorials alone, without hands-on practice or individual mentorship.

But if you look around our campus, seniors and batchmates already possess these exact skills! One student is great at coding; another is great at design.
Skill Swap connects these students through a zero-cost barter credit economy.

On our platform, knowledge is currency. You do not need real money to learn. When you teach someone a skill you know, you earn Credits. You can then spend those credits to learn any skill you want from another student!

We built the frontend using React.js, Vite, and modern CSS, adopting a clean Academic Teal theme designed to feel approachable, human-made, and organized.

To explain how our backend securely manages user data, sessions, and credit balances, I now hand over to Varun."
Judge Q: Why React instead of HTML/JS? → Tulika: "React's modular components allow us to reuse cards, navbars, and modals seamlessly while updating live state with zero page reloads."
2. VARUN — Backend Architecture, Database Schemas & APIs 2:30 – 5:00 (2.5 Mins)
SCREEN CUE: Keep on Landing or show Backend API Health Terminal
"Thank you, Tulika. Hello everyone, my name is Varun, and I will explain the Backend Architecture, Database Design, and RESTful APIs of Skill Swap.

Our backend runs on Node.js using the Express.js framework, connected to a MongoDB database via Mongoose ODM. We followed a clean MVC architecture to ensure modularity, high performance, and security.

We designed four core database schemas:
1. User Schema: Stores student profiles, password hashes, current credit balances, and two distinct arrays: skillsToTeach and skillsToLearn.
2. Session Schema: Tracks booking requests, status lifecycles (requested, accepted, completed), meeting durations, and WebRTC room IDs.
3. Feedback Schema: Captures numerical ratings and evaluation criteria such as clarity, helpfulness, and punctuality.
4. Gamification Schema: Stores unlocked badges, learning streaks, and community rankings.

For security, we implemented JWT (JSON Web Tokens) for stateless authentication and bcrypt.js to salt and hash all passwords.

Through our Learner and Tutor Module APIs, our server safely validates credit balances before confirming any booking, locking the amount in escrow until the session is completed so no double-spending can occur.

Now, to walk you through the actual live student experience and booking flow, I hand over to Tejas."
Judge Q: How do you prevent credit double-spending? → Varun: "Our API verifies credit balances before booking, locks the amount in escrow, and only releases it after session status becomes 'completed'."

4. UJJWAL (Last) — WebRTC Video Room, AI Matching & Conclusion 7:30 – 10:00 (2.5 Mins)
SCREEN CUES: 1. Click Screen 7 (Video Call) → 2. Click Screen 8 (Leaderboard)
"Thank you, Tejas. Hello everyone, I'm Ujjwal, and I will be explaining our Real-Time WebRTC Video System, AI Skill Recommendation Engine, and Gamification.

(Click Screen 7: Video Room Page)
Unlike traditional platforms that force students to switch to Zoom or Google Meet, Skill Swap features native in-browser video calling powered by WebRTC.
WebRTC streams encrypted audio and video directly peer-to-peer between browsers with near-zero latency. Our Node.js server acts as the Signaling Server, negotiating SDP handshakes and ICE candidates.
As you can see in Screen 7's theater mode, we have a running elapsed session timer, dual camera tiles for tutor and learner, floating call controls (mic, camera, screen share), and a live notes and chat sidebar.

AI Skill Matching Engine:
To intelligently match learners with the right tutors, we designed a Python microservice with Scikit-learn. It computes cosine similarity between a learner’s wanted skills and tutor profiles, ranking mentors by relevance, past ratings, and credibility.

(Click Screen 8: Leaderboard Page)
Finally, to sustain student motivation, our Gamification Engine rewards active participation with badges like First Step, Helper, Consistent, and Explorer.
Whenever a session ends and feedback is submitted, the tutor's AI Credibility Score automatically recalculates based on weighted peer ratings.

In conclusion, Skill Swap is a complete, self-sustaining peer ecosystem that makes skill learning democratic, free, and accessible to every student.

Thank you so much for your time, and our team is now ready for any questions!"
Judge Q: Why use WebRTC over Zoom links? → Ujjwal: "WebRTC connects the two browsers directly with peer-to-peer encryption, eliminating third-party accounts and meeting link friction."
